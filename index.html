📖 Setup & Help Guide ▼
1 Plug into Car & Power ON
Insert the controller into your vehicle's OBD2 port (under the steering wheel) and power on the car (READY mode).
2 Choose Wi-Fi Connection
🔹 Option A: Personal Hotspot (Recommended)
Turn on your phone's Personal Hotspot:
Network: BMS Password: bmslocal
The controller links automatically in ~10 seconds.
🔸 Option B: Direct Controller Wi-Fi
Connect phone to controller's Wi-Fi network:
Network: BMS_Setup Password: bmslocal
Open http://192.168.4.1 to configure custom car Wi-Fi.
3 Launch Dashboard or Companion App
Tap «🚀 Open Dashboard» above, or use the BMS Companion Android APK below.
🔴 Dashboard not opening or offline?
• Ensure vehicle is in READY mode.
• Verify phone hotspot name is BMS and password is bmslocal.
• On iPhone, turn ON «Maximize Compatibility» in Personal Hotspot settings.
📶 Tips for BMS_Setup Wi-Fi:
• Turn OFF «Auto-reconnect» for BMS_Setup.
• If prompted «No Internet», tap «Keep Wi-Fi connection» / «Always connect».
🟡 IP address changed?
• Connect to BMS_Setup and tap «Auto-Sync via BMS_Setup».
• Or check phone Hotspot settings for the connected device IP and save it manually.
⚙️ Reconfigure or reset Wi-Fi?
Turn off phone hotspot for 15s. The controller will open BMS_Setup hotspot at 192.168.4.1.
