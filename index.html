Help & FAQ
Knowledge base
1 Turn ON Phone Hotspot
Enable Personal Hotspot on your phone with the following credentials:
Network: Bms Password: bmslocal
⚠️ Important: Only supports 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. On iPhone, turn ON «Maximize Compatibility».
2 Plug into Car & Power ON
Insert the controller into your vehicle's OBD2 port (under the steering wheel) and power on the car (READY mode).
3 IP Discovery (Auto-Sync)
Connect phone Wi-Fi to controller network with the following credentials:
Network: Bms_Setup Password: bmslocal
Then tap the button:
The controller will send its IP to Live Radar, disable Bms_Setup, and redirect you back with IP saved.
Для продвинутых пользователей
Где взять IP-адрес? В настройках точки доступа телефона перейдите в раздел «Подключенные устройства». Нажмите «Ввести IP вручную» и сохраните его.
4 Launch Live Monitor
After obtaining the IP address, tap «📊 Live Monitor» above to view live telemetry.
🛡️ How does autonomy and updates work? ▼
• ⚡ Full autonomy: Controller is plugged into the vehicle and automatically regulates fan speed. A discharged or forgotten phone never affects battery cooling.
• 📲 Energy-efficient link: Phone battery is not drained by continuous data polling. Connection is only active when viewing battery temperature or changing network settings.
• 🔄 Support & updates: Both controller and mobile app are engineered with support for wireless updates.
🔴 Dashboard not opening or offline? ▼
• Ensure vehicle is in READY mode.
• Verify hotspot is ON: default network Bms (password bmslocal) or your custom network.
• On iPhone, turn ON «Maximize Compatibility» in Personal Hotspot settings.
• On Android, set Hotspot AP Band to 2.4 GHz.
⚙️ How to connect controller to custom/home Wi-Fi? ▼
1. Turn off phone hotspot for 15s. The controller will launch its setup Wi-Fi Bms_Setup (password bmslocal).
2. Connect phone Wi-Fi to Bms_Setup, open http://10.10.10.1 or tap «Auto-Sync» above.
3. In the Wi-Fi settings section, enter your hotspot SSID and password, then save.
The controller will reconnect to your network automatically!
❓ Changed phone or typo in Wi-Fi password? ▼
No worries: turn off phone hotspot for 15 seconds. Since the controller cannot find the network, it will re-open Bms_Setup (password bmslocal), so you can enter new credentials via http://10.10.10.1.
📶 Tips for Bms_Setup Wi-Fi: ▼
• Turn OFF «Auto-reconnect» for Bms_Setup.
• If prompted «No Internet», tap «Keep Wi-Fi connection» / «Always connect».
🤖 Android Hotspot 2.4GHz Settings: ▼
In your Android phone settings ➔ Portable Hotspot / Tethering ➔ Set up hotspot, make sure AP Band is set to 2.4 GHz Band (not 5.0 GHz preferred).
🟡 IP address changed? ▼
• Connect phone Wi-Fi to Bms_Setup and tap «Auto-Sync via Bms_Setup».
• Or check phone Hotspot settings for the connected device IP and save it manually.
