📖 Setup & Help Guide ▼
1 Plug into Car & Power ON
Insert the controller into your vehicle's OBD2 port (under the steering wheel) and power on the car (READY or ON mode).
2 Choose Wi-Fi Connection
🔹 Option A: Quick Start (Recommended)
Turn on your phone's Personal Hotspot:
Network: BMS Password: bmslocal
The controller links automatically in ~10 seconds.
🔸 Option B: Custom In-Car Wi-Fi / Router
If you use an in-car modem or custom hotspot:
1. Connect phone Wi-Fi to BMS_Setup (password: bmslocal).
2. Important: Turn OFF «Auto-reconnect» in Wi-Fi settings. If prompted «Internet not available», select «Always connect» / «Stay connected».
3. Open http://192.168.4.1 in your browser.
4. Enter your custom Wi-Fi name & password and tap Save & Connect.
3 Link & Launch Dashboard
Tap «📡 Auto-Sync via BMS_Setup» above to detect the controller, then tap «🚀 Open Dashboard» to view real-time battery temperatures & fan controls.
🔴 Dashboard not opening or device offline?
• Ensure the vehicle is powered on (READY or ON mode).
• Verify your phone's Personal Hotspot is active with name BMS and password bmslocal.
• On iPhone, enable «Maximize Compatibility» in Personal Hotspot settings.
📶 Tips when connecting to BMS_Setup:
• Disable Auto-reconnect: When entering password bmslocal, turn OFF the «Auto-reconnect» toggle so your phone doesn't stay locked to the setup network.
• No Internet prompt: If your phone warns «Internet may not be available», choose «Always connect» / «Keep Wi-Fi connection».
🟡 IP address changed?
• Connect phone Wi-Fi to BMS_Setup (password: bmslocal) and tap «Auto-Sync via BMS_Setup».
• Or check Settings ➔ Personal Hotspot ➔ Connected Devices on your phone to find the new IP address and enter it manually.
⚙️ Reconfigure or reset Wi-Fi?
Turn off your phone hotspot for 15 seconds. The controller will launch its own BMS_Setup hotspot so you can access 192.168.4.1 and reconfigure it.
